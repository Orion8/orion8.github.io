# القرآن — The Qur’an / 쿠란

V3 integratedLayout; sequence 22; catalogue id 16; prepared 2026-10-20.

## Evidence and scope
- https://quran.com/ — primary-text index, 114 surahs, separate Surah/Juz/Revelation Order navigation.
- https://quran.com/1 — Al-Fatihah, prayer for guidance, mercy and judgment.
- https://quran.com/112 — divine unity.
- https://quran.com/2/177 — faith, charity, prayer, promises and patience.
- https://quran.com/4/135 — justice even against self or relatives.
- https://quran.com/16/102 — revelation described within the text.
- https://quran.net/read — corroborating 114-surah / 30-juz index.

The thematic chain is explicitly a reader's synthesis, not official divisions. Thirty juz are reading divisions, not chapters. Received ordering is not chronological; no claim of strictly descending lengths. Verse totals and revelation dates are intentionally omitted. Attribution states Islamic belief in revelation to Muhammad, not Muhammad's authorship. No depiction of God or prophets. Korean and English explanations are original paraphrases, not quotations from one translation. Manuscripts, indexed pages and books are explanatory illustrations, not facsimiles or an edition's volume count.
