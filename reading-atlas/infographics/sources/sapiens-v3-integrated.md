# Sapiens — V3 integrated infographic

## Bibliographic baseline

- Original Hebrew title: קיצור תולדות האנושות
- English title: *Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind*
- Author: Yuval Noah Harari
- First published in Hebrew in 2011; the Reading Atlas card retains the original title.

## Verified structure

The infographic follows the official four-part, twenty-chapter structure:

1. The Cognitive Revolution — chapters 1–4
2. The Agricultural Revolution — chapters 5–8
3. The Unification of Humankind — chapters 9–13
4. The Scientific Revolution — chapters 14–20

The central visual model summarizes the book's recurring explanatory chain:
shared fictions / imagined orders → trust among strangers → flexible large-scale cooperation.

The four provocations are explicitly labeled as the author's interpretive claims rather than uncontested historical facts. The reading questions invite evidence checking, attention to omitted experience, and separation of collective power from well-being and freedom.
