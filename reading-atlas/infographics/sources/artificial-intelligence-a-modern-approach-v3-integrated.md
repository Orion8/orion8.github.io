## Structural verification

The infographic follows the official fourth-edition organization: seven parts and 28 chapters. The seven-part map is Artificial Intelligence; Problem-solving; Knowledge, reasoning, and planning; Uncertain knowledge and reasoning; Machine learning; Communicating, perceiving, and acting; Conclusions.

The book's organizing model is the rational agent: percepts inform internal state and knowledge; reasoning and learning support action selection; actions change the environment and generate further percepts. Search, logic, probability, utility, learning, language, vision, robotics, ethics, and safety are presented as complementary components of that loop.
