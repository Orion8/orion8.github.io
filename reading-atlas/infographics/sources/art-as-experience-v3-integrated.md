# Art as Experience · V3 integrated infographic sources

- John Dewey, *Art as Experience* (1934).
- The original book contains 14 chapters. The infographic groups them as 1–3 (organism, environment, having an experience), 4–8 (expression, object, form, energy), 9–11 (media and human contribution), and 12–14 (philosophy, criticism, civilization).
- Dewey's model is rendered as organism ↔ environment, doing ↔ undergoing, and movement through resistance, tension, accumulation, rhythm, and consummation.
- “Consummation” is distinguished from mere termination; expression is distinguished from immediate discharge.
- The current Korean Books World / Lee Jae-eon edition is an excerpt translation of original chapters 1–3, not a full translation of all 14 chapters.
