## Core claims represented

- The scientific target is the biological basis of conscious experience.
- Perception is predictive inference: top-down predictions are continually constrained by sensory evidence and prediction error.
- The experienced self is not one indivisible entity but a collection of embodied perceptual processes.
- Interoceptive prediction and regulation of the living body are central to the beast-machine account of selfhood.
- Animal and machine consciousness require evidence and criteria rather than surface resemblance alone.
