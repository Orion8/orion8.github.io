## Accuracy boundaries

This is a collection of essays and lectures, not a textbook with twenty steps. The four-stage problem/conjecture/test/criticism loop is a reader's conceptual synthesis, not a claimed formal diagram or verbatim formula from the book. The return is to new problems, not guaranteed final certainty.

Surviving severe tests is not proof of truth. Falsifiability is a demarcation proposal, not a claim that every non-scientific statement is meaningless. A contrary observation is not an automatic, unreviewable practical refutation: measurements, observation reports and auxiliary assumptions also need scrutiny. Critical discussion extends beyond empirical science without making every political or philosophical claim an experimental law. We omit numerical truthlikeness measures, whose original formal definitions later encountered problems.

The astronomy illustrations are conceptual metaphors, not reported observations, named astronomical findings or quantitatively accurate charts. A targeted Korean edit makes its observation/prediction comparison depict the same kind of spiral galaxy rather than unrelated celestial objects.
