## Verified structure

The infographic follows the official three-part, twenty-chapter structure:

1. Part I — Applied Math and Machine Learning Basics (chapters 1–5)
2. Part II — Deep Networks: Modern Practices (chapters 6–12)
3. Part III — Deep Learning Research (chapters 13–20)

Its visual argument moves from hierarchical representation learning through the forward pass, loss, backpropagation and optimization; then compares feedforward, convolutional and recurrent architectures; and closes with generalization, regularization, autoencoders, probabilistic models and generative models.
