# The Epic of Gilgamesh — V3 integrated infographic

Prepared 2026-10-21 for Reading Atlas sequence 25 (catalogue id 19).

## Edition and structure baseline

- The poster uses the Standard Babylonian twelve-tablet arrangement as the structural baseline.
- Tablets I–XI form the continuous narrative: Gilgamesh and Enkidu; the Cedar Forest and Humbaba; the Bull of Heaven; Enkidu's death; Gilgamesh's journey; Utnapishtim, the flood, the plant, and the return to Uruk.
- Tablet XII is explicitly identified as an appendix-like, separately transmitted Netherworld tradition rather than the narrative continuation of Tablet XI.
- Because the epic survives in multiple versions and incomplete fragments, the poster states that modern translations are reconstructions.

## Visual and text QA

- Korean and English were composed as two independent, self-contained raster posters.
- Each image is 1024 × 1536 WebP and includes all titles, labels, captions, illustrations, arrows, and section numbers inside the image.
- No HTML text overlay, external font dependency, SVG text, or language-swapping layer is used.
- Full-size output and 390 px-wide raster previews were inspected for hierarchy, contrast, spelling, tablet numbering, and mobile-scale legibility.
- The illustrations are integrated with the reading path: clay tablet and Uruk, paired heroes, cedar forest, bull, mourning, waters and flood survivor, snake and plant, and the final return to the city walls.
