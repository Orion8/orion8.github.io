# The Rest Is Noise · V3 integrated infographic sources

- Alex Ross, *The Rest Is Noise: Listening to the Twentieth Century* (2007).
- The original structure is 3 parts and 15 chapters: 1900–1933 (chapters 1–6), 1933–1945 (chapters 7–9), and 1945–2000 (chapters 10–15).
- The central interpretive model is composer ↔ society and politics ↔ sound, with technology, distribution, and audience as connected forces.
- Ross explicitly rejects a simple progressive-versus-conservative teleology and tracks crossings among tonal/atonal, classical/jazz/rock, institutions, markets, war, dictatorship, exile, race, and technology.
- The book is selective rather than encyclopedic; the infographic preserves that caveat.
- Korean edition: Alex Ross; translated by Kim Byeong-hwa; Book21; 2010.
