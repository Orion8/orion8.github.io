## Accuracy and interpretive boundaries

The two top-level doctrines are Elements and Method. Method is not nested inside Elements. Aesthetic concerns space/time; Logic divides into Analytic and Dialectic. Method has Discipline, Canon, Architectonic and History. Prefaces and Introduction precede the two main doctrines; the image is a main-structure map, not a complete contents transcription.

The converging sensibility/understanding diagram is a philosophical explanation of conditions, explicitly not a neuroscience pipeline. Objects are given through intuition; categories synthesize the manifold and enable judgments. The apple sequence illustrates an event and causal order, not a claim that Kant discovered gravity or that categories are learned from apple observations.

The Korean perspective names synthetic a priori judgments; English names the broader a priori question. These are complementary compressed formulations of the same reading focus, not literal word-for-word translations. The question concerns informative judgments with a priori validity, not merely personal assumptions or beliefs held before a particular event. Both maps distinguish appearances from mere illusions and avoid depicting a knowable second secret world. The Dialectic limits speculative claims about soul, world-totality and God; it does not prove their nonexistence or eliminate their regulative/moral roles.
