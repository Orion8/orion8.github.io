# Principia — V3 integrated infographic source note

- Book: Isaac Newton, *Philosophiæ Naturalis Principia Mathematica* (first published 1687). The infographic uses the final 1726 third edition as its structural baseline.
- University of California Press, *The Principia: The Authoritative Translation and Guide*: confirms the three laws of motion, universal gravity, and contents: Definitions, Axioms/Laws, Books 1–2 *The Motion of Bodies*, and Book 3 *The System of the World*. https://www.ucpress.edu/books/the-principia-the-authoritative-translation-and-guide/paper
- Library of Congress, *Principia*: confirms the preliminary Definitions and Laws, three-book structure, and the 1687, 1713, and 1726 lifetime editions. https://www.loc.gov/item/2021667054/
- Newton Project, 1726 front matter: consulted for the final-edition context. https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00084
- University of California Press, Motte translation description: Newton's program runs from phenomena to forces and from forces to further phenomena. https://www.ucpress.edu/books/sir-isaac-newtons-mathematical-principles-of-natural-philosophy-and-his-system-of-the-world

The four structure stops on the image are Definitions/Laws, Book I (motion without resistance), Book II (motion in resisting media), and Book III (the system of the world). The English poster's gravity equation is modern symbolic shorthand, not a claim that Newton printed that notation. The same core content was independently composed in Korean and English as two self-contained raster images. At 390-pixel display width both complete images were visually reviewed. Legacy and V2 assets were retained.
