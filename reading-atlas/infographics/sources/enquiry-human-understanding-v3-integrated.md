# An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding — V3 integrated bilingual anatomy

Prepared 2026-10-19. Catalogue id 12; original production sequence 18.

## Sources and accuracy

- https://davidhume.org/texts/e/ — primary text contents: twelve sections.
- https://davidhume.org/texts/e/notes — Peter Millican's editorial notes: first published 1748 as Philosophical Essays Concerning Human Understanding; renamed in 1758. The header date is first publication, not the first use of the later title.
- https://davidhume.org/texts/e/2 — impressions, ideas, and testing an idea by tracing its impression. The reading question is not a claim that every complex idea is a literal sensory copy; Hume discusses the missing shade exception.
- https://davidhume.org/texts/e/4 — relations of ideas versus matters of fact; causal inference beyond observation; the circularity of using past success to prove that the future must resemble the past.
- https://davidhume.org/texts/e/5 — custom or habit explains expectation after repeated conjunction. This is a psychological account, not a deductive justification of induction.
- https://davidhume.org/texts/e/7 — necessary connection is not directly perceived as an external power. This does not mean that physical causes do not exist.
- https://davidhume.org/texts/e/12 — mitigated scepticism, modesty and bounds of enquiry; not abandonment of ordinary life or empirical investigation.

The three reading groups (1–3, 4–7, 8–12) are explicitly reader-supplied, not formal parts. Sections 8–12 cover liberty and necessity, animal reasoning, miracles, particular providence and a future state, and sceptical philosophy. The compressed English map says Liberty; the Korean map spells out liberty and necessity. Both depict the same intellectual route without claiming to reproduce every formal heading.
