# The Society of Mind — V3 integrated infographic notes

## Verified structure and thesis

- The publisher describes the book as a puzzle-like mosaic of compact, self-contained chapters and frames its central question as how the mind works.
- The Korean edition's table of contents lists 30 chapters, from “Prologue” and “Wholes and Parts” through memory, learning, perception, consciousness, emotion, language, frames, and “Mental Models.” Its description identifies 270 short essays.
- The core theory proposes that intelligence can be understood through interactions among many simple, individually mindless agents organized into larger agencies. This is presented as Minsky's explanatory lens, not as a settled anatomical map of the brain.
- The builder/block-tower example and the K-line memory idea are grounded in the book's opening material and MIT course summary.
