# The Story of Art · 서양미술사 — V3 integrated layout

## Bibliographic baseline

- E. H. Gombrich, *The Story of Art*. First published 1950; poster follows Phaidon's 16th edition (688 pages).
- Structure verified as the Introduction “On Art and Artists” followed by 28 numbered chapters. Chapter 28, “A Story without End,” contains “The Triumph of Modernism,” “Another Turning of the Tide,” and “The Changing Past.”
- Korean edition verified as 예경, *서양미술사*, translated by 백승길 and 이종숭, based on the revised, expanded and redesigned 16th edition (687 pages in the Korean catalog record).

## Interpretive model used in the infographic

The poster treats Gombrich's narrative as a sequence of artists answering inherited tasks with revised conventions: task → convention → looking/making → new solution → new convention. This is a reader map, not a replacement for the book's own chapter divisions. The seven turning points and five chapter ranges are explicitly presented as navigational groupings.

## Critical framing

The book remains an unusually clear and influential introduction, but its center of gravity is Western European. The final panel therefore asks readers to notice who is centered and which cultures, makers and media are omitted. This caution reflects published criticism of the survey's Eurocentric canon.
