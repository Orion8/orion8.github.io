## Content basis
The Clay Mathematics Institute's digitized Bodleian manuscript identifies the received work as thirteen books and provides book/proposition indexing. David Joyce's Clark University table of contents organizes the propositions by book. The infographic groups these books as a reader's map (I–IV plane geometry; V–VI ratio/similarity; VII–IX number theory; X incommensurable magnitudes; XI–XIII solid geometry), not as Euclid's own named parts.

The proof-flow illustration is explanatory: definitions and first principles support constructions and propositions, with results depending on preceding results. The three landmarks are examples rather than an exhaustive list: I.47 (Pythagorean theorem), I.29 (parallel-line results), and Book XIII (the five regular solids).
