# Complexity: A Guided Tour — V3 integrated infographic notes

## Verified book structure

Oxford Academic lists five official parts and nineteen numbered chapters:

1. Part One, “Background and History” — chapters 1–7
2. Part Two, “Life and Evolution in Computers” — chapters 8–9
3. Part Three, “Computation Writ Large” — chapters 10–14
4. Part Four, “Network Thinking” — chapters 15–18
5. Part Five, “Conclusion” — chapter 19

The graphics reproduce these part and chapter divisions. Korean part names are translations, not alternate official headings.

## Content checks

- The central question and visual thread summarize Mitchell’s account of large-scale organized and adaptive behavior emerging from simple interactions.
- “Local rules + interaction + time” is explicitly identified as an editorial synthesis, not a named equation or formal model in the book.
- The four lenses (dynamics, information/computation, evolution, networks) represent recurring themes across the book; they are not presented as the book’s official part titles.
- Examples shown (army-ant coordination, cellular automata, evolutionary branching, networks) are illustrative of topics treated in the book.
