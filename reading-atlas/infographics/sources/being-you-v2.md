- II: Content — chapters 4–6: Perceiving from the Inside Out; The Wizard of Odds; The Beholder’s Share
- III: Self — chapters 7–11: Delirium; Expect Yourself; Being a Beast Machine; A Fish in Water; Degrees of Freedom
- IV: Other — chapters 12–13: Beyond Human; Machine Minds
- Epilogue

## Core claims represented

- The scientific target is the biological basis and measurable properties of conscious experience.
- Perception is predictive inference: top-down expectations are constrained and corrected by sensory prediction errors.
- The experienced self is not a single indivisible entity, but a collection of embodied perceptual processes.
- Interoceptive prediction and regulation of the living body are central to the “beast machine” account of selfhood.
- Questions about animal and machine consciousness require evidence and criteria, not surface resemblance alone.
