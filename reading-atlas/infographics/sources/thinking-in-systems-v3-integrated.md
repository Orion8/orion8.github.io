# Thinking in Systems — V3 integrated layout

## Verified structure

- Introduction: The Systems Lens
- Part 1, System Structure and Behavior: Chapter 1, The Basics; Chapter 2, A Brief Visit to the Systems Zoo
- Part 2, Systems and Us: Chapters 3–5, Why Systems Work So Well; Why Systems Surprise Us; System Traps … and Opportunities
- Part 3, Creating Change—in Systems and in Our Philosophy: Chapters 6–7, Leverage Points—Places to Intervene in a System; Living in a World of Systems

## Editorial model

- The opening visual moves from event to pattern to underlying structure.
- The central stock–flow illustration shows that a stock changes through inflows and outflows.
- Reinforcing feedback, balancing feedback, and delay are placed directly around the central model so that words, arrows, and illustrations form one reading path.
- The leverage ladder is a compact six-level reading aid. It preserves Meadows's direction from shallow parameters toward deeper rules, goals, and paradigms without claiming to replace her full 12-point list.
- The closing question asks readers to identify the structure that keeps producing an observed result.
