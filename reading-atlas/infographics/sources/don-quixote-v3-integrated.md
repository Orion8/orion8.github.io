## Interpretive boundaries

The part captions are explicitly labeled reader descriptions, not Cervantes's formal section titles. The four-stage stories/interpretation/action/reinterpretation loop is an original reading lens, not a formal psychological theory claimed by Cervantes. The windmill is real in the scene; the translucent giant is an imagined interpretation. The companionship panel avoids reducing Quixote and Sancho to an unchanging idealist/realist binary. The narrator and character perspectives are distinguished; the illustrated narrator is not a documentary portrait. The ending is not disclosed.
