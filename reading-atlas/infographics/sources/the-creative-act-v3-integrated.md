# The Creative Act — V3 integrated infographic sources

## Bibliographic baseline
- Rick Rubin, *The Creative Act: A Way of Being*. Penguin Press, 2023.
- English edition: 432 pages, ISBN 9780593652886.
- Korean edition: *창조적 행위: 존재의 방식*, translated by 정지현, 코쿤북스, 2023, ISBN 9791197831744.

## Structure checked
- The volume is explicitly organized as “78 Areas of Thought.”
- The contents begin with “Everyone Is a Creator” and proceed through attention, awareness, practice, seeds, experimentation, crafting, completion, rules, play, release/regeneration, and related themes.
- The final entries include “Why Make Art?”, “Harmony,” and “What We Tell Ourselves.”
- The poster's SOURCE → AWARENESS → SEEDS → EXPERIMENTATION → CRAFTING → COMPLETION → RELEASE sequence is an editorial synthesis of recurring chapter headings, not a claim that the book labels seven formal parts.

## Interpretive guardrail
This is a philosophical and practical field guide to creative practice. It is not presented as a scientific theory or as a guaranteed formula for success.
