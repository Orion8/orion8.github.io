# 紅樓夢 / Dream of the Red Chamber — source notes

## Sources checked

- [University of Minnesota Libraries Publishing: *Dream of the Red Chamber — Afterlives*](https://open.lib.umn.edu/redchamber/) — a scholarly open textbook by Ann Waltner covering the text, major characters, social world, daily life, gender, adaptations, and reception.
- [University of Minnesota: controversy over the last forty chapters](https://open.lib.umn.edu/redchamber/chapter/controversy-over-the-last-40-chapters/) — manuscript circulation from 1754, the 80/40 seam, the 1791 Cheng–Gao printed edition, unresolved authorship, and the normal use of the 120-chapter text.
- [Australian National University Humanities Research Centre](https://hrc.cass.anu.edu.au/events/books-changed-humanity-story-stonedream-red-chamber) — John Minford on the Penguin translation's traditional division: David Hawkes translated chapters 1–80, and Minford translated 81–120, reflecting the Cao / Gao editorial division.
- [Penguin Classics: *The Story of the Stone* series](https://www.penguin.co.uk/series/STORSTONE/the-story-of-the-stone) — the Jia family's changing fortunes; Baoyu, Daiyu, and Baochai; love, household rivalry, political intrigue, karma, illusion, financial ruin, and awakening.
- [University of Minnesota: Lin Daiyu](https://open.lib.umn.edu/redchamber/chapter/lin-daiyu/) — the Crimson Pearl Flower, the debt of tears, outsider status, flower burial, poetry, and chapter 97 in the 120-chapter ending.
- [University of Minnesota: Xue Baochai](https://open.lib.umn.edu/redchamber/chapter/xue-baochai/) — the gold locket, the gold-and-jade marriage logic, Baochai's social position, marriage, and Baoyu's departure.
- [University of Minnesota: the social world](https://open.lib.umn.edu/redchamber/part/the-social-world-of-the-dream/) and [daily life](https://open.lib.umn.edu/redchamber/chapter/daily-life-in-the-novel/) — Prospect Garden as a gendered social space and the household's labor, money, hierarchy, marriage, and material life.
- [Chinese Text Project: 《紅樓夢》](https://ctext.org/hongloumeng/zh) — complete 120-chapter Chinese reading text and chapter titles.

## Editorial decisions

- The poster preserves the traditional Chinese title 《紅樓夢》 and uses English-only explanatory copy.
- The familiar 120-chapter Cheng–Gao text is the reading baseline because it is the version normally published and read. The poster does not present its final forty chapters as uncontroversially written by Gao E.
- Chapters 1–80 are labeled as the early manuscript tradition attributed to Cao Xueqin; chapters 81–120 are labeled as the 1791 printed completion edited and published by Cheng Weiyuan and Gao E, with disputed authorship.
- The four movement labels within chapters 1–80 and the six-step “Stone's Journey” are reader aids, not author-supplied parts or quoted formulas.
- “Dream of the Red Chamber” is used as the primary English title; “The Story of the Stone” is retained as the major alternative title used by the Penguin translation.
- The poster includes the ending and explicitly marks the textual seam after chapter 80 so the reading experience does not erase the edition problem.
- The illustrated background was generated with no text. All titles, chapter numbers, names, and interpretive copy were composed in a separate SVG layer for accuracy and legibility.
