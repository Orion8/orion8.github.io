## Evidence and scope
- https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/srimad/intro — IIT Kanpur's Gita Supersite introduction: 18 chapters and 700 or 701 shlokas; scripts and commentary resources.
- https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/srimad?choose=1&field_chapter_value=18&field_nsutra_value=66&language=dv&scms=1 — primary-text location 18.66 and commentary interface.
- https://academic.oup.com/reference/62357/reference-article-abstract/554517840 — Oxford Bibliographies overview: Mahabharata Book 6 placement, battlefield crisis, 18 chapters, 700 verses, Chapter 11 turning point and concluding decision.
- https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/286340/the-bhagavad-gita-by-anonymous-translated-by-juan-mascaro-introduction-by-simon-brodbeck/ — publisher description confirming 18 chapters.

The 18 chapters and traditional 700-verse count are the actual structure; IIT Kanpur notes a 700/701 variation, so the poster records both without conflating editions. The karma/jnana/bhakti triangle is explicitly marked as a reading aid rather than the text's formal three-part table of contents. The phrases about action, knowledge, devotion and choice are concise reader-oriented syntheses of recurring arguments and the cited reading coordinates, not quotations from a single translation. The poster avoids reducing the work to a command simply to perform duty.
