## Structural baseline

The official table of contents has 18 chapters and no named parts. The four groupings in the infographic are explicitly labeled a reader's map/editorial grouping, not official divisions:

1. Chapters 1–5: explanations and reality
2. Chapters 6–8: universality and creativity
3. Chapters 9–14: optimism, choice, and the multiverse
4. Chapters 15–18: culture, creativity, and open-ended progress

## Accuracy notes

- Central thesis: theoretical and practical progress results from the quest for good explanations; improving explanations is the regulating principle of successful human endeavor.
- Knowledge is created through conjecture and criticism rather than being passively derived from sensory experience. Experience helps choose among already-created theories.
- "Hard to vary" is used as a compact test for a good explanation: changing its details without spoiling its explanatory work should be difficult.
- Optimism is presented as a stance toward solvable problems, not a promise that progress happens automatically.
- The infographic separates the book's official chapter count from the reader-created thematic map.
