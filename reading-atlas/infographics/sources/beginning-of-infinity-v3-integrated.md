# The Beginning of Infinity — V3 integrated layout

## Verified structure

- The author's official contents list 18 chapters and no named parts.
- Visual reading map (explicitly not official divisions): Chapters 1–5 explanations and reality; 6–8 creativity and universality; 9–14 optimism, choice, and the multiverse; 15–18 culture and open-ended progress.

## Editorial model

- Core question: how knowledge can continue to grow.
- The central cycle connects conjecture, criticism, improved explanations, and new questions. This is a compact synthesis of Deutsch's account of knowledge creation and criticism, not a verbatim quotation.
- The explanation criterion is paraphrased as being hard to vary while retaining explanatory work.
- “Problems are inevitable” and “Problems are solvable” express the paired optimistic stance; the infographic clarifies that progress is not automatic.
- The closing prompt asks what better explanations make understandable or changeable.
