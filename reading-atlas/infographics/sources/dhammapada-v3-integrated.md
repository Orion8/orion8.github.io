## Editorial interpretation

- “Intention → speech and action → habit → suffering or freedom” is a compact explanatory model distilled from recurring verse relationships. It is not presented as a quotation.
- The four-stage “Notice → Choose → Repeat → Release” path is an original reading aid, not an original division of the text.
- The poster explicitly labels its thematic flow as a reader's map. The historical structure remains 26 chapters and 423 verses.
- The eight named chapters are anchor examples, not a complete table of contents.

## Visual and text QA

- Korean and English were composed as independent self-contained raster images using the same conceptual grid.
- The Korean chapter-anchor typo was corrected from `깨여 있음` to `깨어 있음` before export.
- Both WebP files were inspected at full size and as complete 390-pixel-wide raster previews.
- No HTML text overlay, SVG text, external font dependency, or language-swapping layer is used in the final web assets.
- At a whole-poster 390-pixel preview, headings and the core flow remain scannable; small supporting sentences require pinch zoom.
