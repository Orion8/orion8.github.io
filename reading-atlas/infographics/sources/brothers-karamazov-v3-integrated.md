## Accuracy and interpretation

The contents comprise four parts, each containing three books (I–III, IV–VI, VII–IX, X–XII), followed by a three-chapter epilogue. The infographic's short descriptions of those parts are explicitly labeled reader summaries, not original part titles. Korean 편 is used for the twelve internal books to distinguish them from the four parts.

The four character lenses and the legal/moral responsibility split are an interpretive reading guide, not a formal model proposed by the novelist. They do not exhaust the characters' personalities. Character speech is distinguished from an authorial verdict. The identity of the murderer and the trial outcome are not disclosed. Illustrations are interpretive, not documentary portraits of fictional people.
