## Accuracy and interpretive boundaries

The structure shown is seven generations intertwined with the rise and fall of Macondo. Founding, civil war, boom/violence, and decline are explicitly a reader's four-movement guide, NOT formal parts of the book. No chapter-count claim, invented part titles, detailed genealogy, or historical casualty count is printed.

The open spiral is an editorial reading lens, not a formula stated by García Márquez or a deterministic causal model. Repeated names do not imply identical fates: both images explicitly ask readers to notice differences and historical change. Memory/power and love/solitude are interpretive questions, not an alleged single thesis of the novel. Train, banana company, family house, and manuscripts are narrative motifs; yellow butterflies and floating pages/sheets are illustrative motifs, not literal genealogy markers. Mild thematic spoilers; no ending spelled out.
