## Accuracy boundaries

Baseline is the commonly read 120-chapter text. Images explicitly distinguish first 80 chapters and last 40 and state that authorship/completion of the latter remains disputed. They do not assert Gao E definitively wrote every late chapter, and do not confuse a textual seam with a formal plot division. Korean 정위원·고악 corresponds to Cheng Weiyuan / Gao E. Original title 紅樓夢 retained; author 조설근 / Cao Xueqin; no spurious exact composition date.

The three-layer feeling → household order → dream/impermanence diagram is expressly a reader's lens, not an author-quoted model or a deterministic causal law. Symbols and scenes are conceptual illustrations, not a complete genealogy or precise reconstruction of a documented historical building. No opera-specific plot modification is treated as the novel. The 80/40 manuscript stacks are illustrative, not a quantitative chart. Text inside miniature illustrated manuscript pages is decorative texture, not cited novel excerpts.
