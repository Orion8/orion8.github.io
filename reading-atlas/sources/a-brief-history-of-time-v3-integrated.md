# A Brief History of Time — V3 integrated-layout source note

## Edition baseline

- Poster structure follows Stephen Hawking's original 1988 book: 11 chapters from **Our Picture of the Universe** through **Conclusion**.
- The 1996 illustrated edition updated the original chapters and added a new chapter, **Wormholes and Time Travel**. The poster identifies this as an edition note rather than counting it in the 1988 map.
- The Estate's later-edition description is used only to flag updated discussions of the no-boundary proposal, dark energy, the information paradox, eternal inflation, microwave-background observations, and gravitational waves; these are not presented as part of the original 1988 structure.

## Verified conceptual arc

1. Historical pictures of the cosmos and the move from classical mechanics to relativity.
2. Expansion of the universe, quantum uncertainty, elementary particles, and fundamental forces.
3. Gravitational collapse, event horizons, and Hawking radiation.
4. The origin and possible fate of the universe, the no-boundary proposal, and arrows of time.
5. The unresolved search for a theory that unifies general relativity with quantum mechanics.

## Reading cautions

- The infographic presents claims in the context of Hawking's 1988 popular account. It invites readers to separate observations from model assumptions and to check how the field has changed since publication.
- “No boundary” is presented as a proposal about initial conditions, not as settled observational fact.
- Hawking radiation is shown as the book's central quantum-black-hole result; the schematic particle-pair picture is a teaching aid, not a literal microscopic derivation.
