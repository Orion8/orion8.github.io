## Editorial model

The infographic follows one alignment path: human intent is translated into a measurable proxy, a learned model, an action, and real-world feedback. It connects the book’s three parts to prediction bias and interpretability; reinforcement, reward shaping and specification gaming; and imitation, inverse reinforcement learning, preference learning and uncertainty. The closing questions ask who defines the target, whose history enters the data, and who can contest and correct a system.
