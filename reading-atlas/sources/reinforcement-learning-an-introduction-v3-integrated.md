# Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction — v3 integrated layout

## Edition and structure

- Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*, second edition (The MIT Press, 2018).
- The book is organized in three parts and 17 chapters: Part I, Tabular Solution Methods (chapters 1–8); Part II, Approximate Solution Methods (chapters 9–13); Part III, Looking Deeper (chapters 14–17).
- The site card preserves the original 1998 publication year, while the infographic explicitly analyzes the expanded 2018 second edition.

## Editorial model

The composition follows one agent through a gridworld. Its vertical reading path integrates the book map, the agent–environment loop, return and value, temporal-difference learning, exploration versus exploitation, and the move from tables to function approximation and policy gradients. Korean and English were independently typeset as self-contained raster posters.

## Checked concepts

- An agent interacts with an uncertain environment and seeks to maximize cumulative reward.
- Policy, return, value, discounting, TD error, and bootstrapping follow the second edition's notation.
- The contrast between Monte Carlo and TD learning, and between SARSA and Q-learning, is presented as a reading map rather than a complete algorithm specification.
- Reward is described as a goal signal; the closing questions direct attention to state representation, reward specification, and exploration.
