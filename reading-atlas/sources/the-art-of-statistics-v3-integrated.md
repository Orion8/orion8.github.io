## Verified structure

The poster follows the book's fourteen-chapter structure, grouped into four reading movements for legibility:

1. **Ask and display data** — proportions; summaries; populations and measurement.
2. **From relationships to causes** — causation; regression; algorithms, analytics, and prediction.
3. **Quantify uncertainty** — estimates and intervals; probability; probability with statistics; testing and discoveries; Bayesian learning.
4. **Do statistics better** — failure modes; better statistical practice; conclusion.

The central workflow is the PPDAC cycle: **Problem → Plan → Data → Analysis → Conclusion**, returning to a refined problem.

## Editorial interpretation

The composition treats statistics as a chain of questions rather than a catalogue of formulas. The main claim is that data do not interpret themselves: trustworthy conclusions depend on the question, measurement, design, assumptions, uncertainty, and communication. The diagrams distinguish association from causation, model training from testing, intervals from point estimates, prior-to-posterior updating, and common failure modes such as misleading axes, overfitting, and small samples.
