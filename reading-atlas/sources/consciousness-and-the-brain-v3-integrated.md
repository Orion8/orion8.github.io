## Poster synthesis

The infographic follows the book's experimental arc: operationalize conscious access, contrast visible and subliminal processing, identify reproducible neural signatures, explain the global neuronal workspace model, and apply the framework to patients, infants, animals, and machines.

The central causal diagram is:

`local processing → competition and threshold → frontoparietal ignition → global broadcast`

The four signatures shown are sudden frontoparietal ignition, the late P3 wave around 300 ms, a late burst of high-frequency activity, and long-distance synchronization.
