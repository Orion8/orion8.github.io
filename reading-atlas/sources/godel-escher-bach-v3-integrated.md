## Editorial structure used

The poster follows the book's two-part, twenty-chapter architecture. It treats the alternating dialogues and expository chapters as a braided composition linking Gödel's formal systems, Escher's recursive figures, and Bach's contrapuntal structures. The explanatory path moves from formal rules and the MU-puzzle through isomorphism and self-reference to incompleteness, levels of description, and the strange loop.
