## Verified structure

The poster follows the book's eleven chapters: Questions; Combination and Structure; Phenomena; Domains; Engineering and Its Solutions; The Origin of Technologies; Structural Deepening; Revolutions and Redomainings; The Mechanisms of Evolution; The Economy Evolving as Its Technologies Evolve; and Where Do We Stand with This Creation of Ours?

## Core model

Arthur's three fundamental principles provide the visual spine:

1. All technologies are combinations of technologies.
2. Every component is itself a technology.
3. A technology captures and organizes natural phenomena for a purpose.

The poster represents this as `phenomenon → principle → component → system → purpose`. Existing technologies become building blocks for new technologies, which in turn become building blocks for later generations. Arthur calls this recursive process combinatorial evolution. Domains supply shared bodies of practices and possibilities; structural deepening adds subsystems and controls; redomaining changes the body of technology used to achieve a purpose. Technology and the economy then reshape one another.
