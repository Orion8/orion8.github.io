# The Demon-Haunted World — V3 integrated layout source note

## Bibliographic baseline

- Original title: *The Demon-Haunted World: Science as a Candle in the Dark*
- Primary author: Carl Sagan. The 1995 first edition credits Carl Sagan; four late chapters are catalogued as written with Ann Druyan. The current Penguin Random House edition lists Carl Sagan and Ann Druyan.
- First edition: Random House, 1995; 25 chapters, followed by acknowledgments, references, and index.
- Korean display title: `악령이 출몰하는 세상` (paired with the unchanged original title).

## Verified structure

The published contents move through five reader-facing arcs used in the poster:

1. **Wonder and the scientific habit** — chapters 1–2.
2. **Claims, memory, and misperception** — chapters 3–9, including the Face on Mars, aliens, secrecy, hallucinations, visions, and therapy.
3. **Testing explanations** — chapters 10–13, including the invisible dragon, grief, the Baloney Detection Kit, and reality-testing.
4. **Skepticism with wonder** — chapters 14–18, including antiscience, scientific responsibility, and the marriage of skepticism and wonder.
5. **Education, freedom, and citizenship** — chapters 19–25, connecting questioning, literacy, science education, witch hunts, and democratic freedom.

These five arcs are an editorial reading map, not headings printed in the book.

## Core claims represented

- Science is both a body of knowledge and a disciplined way of testing claims.
- Skepticism and wonder are complements: openness without testing invites credulity, while skepticism without wonder becomes sterile.
- Anecdote, suggestion, memory, authority, and emotional need can make false explanations feel compelling.
- The Baloney Detection Kit asks for independent confirmation, informed debate, alternative hypotheses, quantification, complete chains of reasoning, simpler adequate explanations, falsifiability, reproducibility, and controlled tests.
- Scientific literacy is a civic capacity: citizens need it to question authority and make decisions in a technological democracy.

## Poster design logic

- A single candle-like vertical path links all sections, but no scenic background dominates the page.
- Evidence tools, case-study illustrations, arrows, labels, and prose share one grid from the outset.
- Korean and English are independently typeset as complete raster posters; neither is a language-swapped background.
- The final web assets contain no HTML/SVG/runtime text overlay.
