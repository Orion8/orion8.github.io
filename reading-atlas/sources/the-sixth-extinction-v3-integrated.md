## Visual/content model

The poster uses a geological tree-of-life timeline interrupted by the Big Five mass-extinction bottlenecks, followed by a present Anthropocene bottleneck. A 13-chapter evidence chain links emblematic species to the drivers acting today: habitat loss and fragmentation, overharvest, fossil-fuel carbon dioxide, warming and ocean acidification, global transport, invasive species, and pathogens. The closing section preserves the book's tension that humans are both the cause of the crisis and capable of deliberate conservation.
