## Editorial structure used

The poster follows the book's eleven chapters plus conclusion. Its visual spine is Strogatz's “Infinity Principle”: divide a continuous problem into infinitely many pieces, solve the easier problems on those pieces, and recombine them. The historical path runs from Archimedes through Galileo, Kepler, Newton, Leibniz, and Fourier to modern applications such as medical imaging, HIV models, and gravitational waves. Differentiation and integration are presented as the two complementary operations joined by the fundamental theorem of calculus.
