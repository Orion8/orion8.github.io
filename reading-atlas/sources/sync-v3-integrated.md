## Verified structure

The infographic follows the book's three-part, ten-chapter progression: biological synchronization; the discovery and mathematical explanation of synchronization; and its exploration through chaos, three-dimensional waves, small-world networks, and human groups.

## Core model

The coupled-oscillator panel uses the standard mean-field Kuramoto model:

`dθᵢ/dt = ωᵢ + (K/N) Σⱼ sin(θⱼ − θᵢ)`

The coherence panel uses the order parameter:

`r = |(1/N) Σⱼ e^(iθⱼ)|`

Here `K` is coupling strength and `r` runs from near 0 (incoherence) to 1 (phase alignment). The small-world section summarizes the Watts–Strogatz result: a network can combine high local clustering with short global paths.
