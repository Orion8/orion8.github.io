# The Big Picture — V3 integrated infographic source notes

## Bibliographic baseline

- **Title:** *The Big Picture: On the Origins of Life, Meaning, and the Universe Itself*
- **Author:** Sean Carroll
- **Publisher / publication:** Dutton, 2016
- **Reading Atlas sequence:** 32 (catalogue id 26)

## Verified structure

Sean Carroll's final official table of contents contains a prologue, **six parts and 50 numbered chapters**, followed by an appendix:

1. **Cosmos (1–8):** law-governed nature, the universe, time's arrow and causation
2. **Understanding (9–18):** evidence, Bayesian updating, emergence, uncertainty and competing explanations
3. **Essence (19–27):** quantum mechanics, the Core Theory, effective theories, body and soul, death
4. **Complexity (28–36):** energy flow, self-organization, life's origin, evolution and emergent purpose
5. **Thinking (37–44):** mind, consciousness, the hard problem, agency and choice
6. **Caring (45–50):** meaning, morality, value and existential orientation

## Editorial synthesis used in the poster

The poster's central ascent — **quantum fields → atoms → cells → minds → values** — is an editorial diagram of Carroll's poetic naturalism, not a literal chapter title. It expresses the book's claim that there is one natural world while different descriptive vocabularies can capture real higher-level patterns.

The second flow — **low entropy → time's arrow → energy flow → self-organization → evolution** — synthesizes the book's movement from cosmology to complexity and life. The Bayesian panel reflects Part Two's chapters on learning, updating knowledge and accepting uncertainty.

The final panel marks the distinction between descriptive science and constructed human value. Claims about the soul, death, consciousness and morality are presented as **the author's naturalist philosophical argument**, not as unqualified scientific consensus.
