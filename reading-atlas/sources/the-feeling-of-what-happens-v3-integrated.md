3. Part III — A Biology for Knowing: “The Organism and the Object”; “The Making of Core Consciousness”; “Extended Consciousness”; “The Neurology of Consciousness”
4. Part IV — Bound to Know: “Feeling Feelings”; “Using Consciousness”; “Under the Light”

## Editorial synthesis used in the poster

- Emotion is an organism-level action program; feeling is the mental perception of the changed body state.
- Core consciousness arises as a nonverbal second-order account of how an object changes the organism.
- The proto-self maps the body's current state; the transient core self is repeatedly rebuilt as objects modify that state.
- Autobiographical memory extends the self across past and anticipated future, enabling extended consciousness.
- Clinical dissociations—including coma and disorders of consciousness, transient global amnesia, anosognosia, and asomatognosia—help distinguish these layers.
