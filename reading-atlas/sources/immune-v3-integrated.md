## Core explanatory sequence used in the poster

1. Barrier defenses establish the boundary of self: skin, mucosa, epithelial cells.
2. Tissue damage triggers innate immunity: macrophages, neutrophils, complement, inflammation.
3. Dendritic cells carry antigen information to lymph nodes and activate T cells.
4. Adaptive immunity coordinates B cells, antibodies, killer T cells, and memory cells.
5. Vaccination creates immune memory through a much safer training exposure.
6. Health depends on regulation and homeostasis; underreaction and overreaction produce different pathologies.

## Scientific guardrails

- The immune system is a distributed network, not a single organ or a simple army.
- Inflammation is protective in context but can damage tissue when excessive or chronic.
- “Boosting” immunity is not a general scientific goal; regulation, specificity, tolerance, and balance matter.
- Vaccination is described as memory-building training, not as risk-free or identical to natural infection.
