## Verified structure and claims

- Five parts, 38 chapters:
  1. Two Systems — chapters 1–9
  2. Heuristics and Biases — chapters 10–18
  3. Overconfidence — chapters 19–24
  4. Choices — chapters 25–34
  5. Two Selves — chapters 35–38
- System 1 is fast, automatic, associative, and emotional; System 2 is slower, effortful, deliberative, and logical.
- The book examines anchoring, availability, question substitution, base-rate neglect, the outside view, overconfidence, prospect theory, loss aversion, framing, the experiencing self, the remembering self, the peak-end rule, and duration neglect.
- The fourfold pattern is represented correctly:
  - high-probability gain: risk averse
  - high-probability loss: risk seeking
  - low-probability gain: risk seeking
  - low-probability loss: risk averse
- The conditions for trustworthy skilled intuition are summarized from Kahneman and Klein (2009): a sufficiently predictable environment and adequate opportunity to learn its regularities through practice and feedback.
