## Core argument used in the infographic

- Energy is not an afterthought to heredity: proton gradients across membranes are universal to cellular life.
- Natural proton gradients in alkaline hydrothermal vents offer a geochemical route to early metabolism before cells could pump their own gradients.
- Bacteria and archaea remained morphologically simple under energetic constraints.
- A singular endosymbiosis joined a bacterial cell with an archaeal host; the bacterium became the mitochondrion and changed the energy available per gene.
- Lane uses that energetic framework to connect the origin of eukaryotic complexity with the nucleus, sex, germline/soma distinction, ageing and death.
- The final part presents predictions and trade-offs, not settled certainty; the poster therefore keeps a clear boundary between explanatory framework and testable hypothesis.
