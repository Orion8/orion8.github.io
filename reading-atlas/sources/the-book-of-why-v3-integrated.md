## Content model used in the infographic

- The 2018 first edition comprises an introduction and ten chapters.
- The central model is the three-level ladder of causation: association/seeing, intervention/doing, and counterfactuals/imagining.
- Causal diagrams encode assumptions that let analysts distinguish observation from intervention, choose valid adjustment sets, identify back-door paths, and avoid collider bias.
- The reading lens emphasizes that causal conclusions require structural assumptions in addition to data, and that higher rungs of the ladder require stronger assumptions.
