## Verified structure

The official table of contents groups 50 chapters into seven parts: foundations (1–3), data compression (4–7), noisy-channel coding (8–11), more information theory (12–19), probabilities and inference (20–37), neural networks (38–46), and sparse graph codes (47–50).

The poster follows MacKay's unifying arc: quantify surprise and uncertainty; compress predictable data; communicate reliably through noise; perform Bayesian inference; interpret learning as probabilistic inference; and recover global messages through local updates in sparse factor graphs.
