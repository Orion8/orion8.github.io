## Verified structure

The first Scribner edition is organized as a prologue, six chronological parts,
and an epilogue:

1. The “Missing Science of Heredity” (1865–1935)
2. “In the Sum of the Parts, There Are Only the Parts” (1930–1970)
3. “The Dreams of Geneticists” (1970–2001)
4. “The Proper Study of Mankind Is Man” (1970–2005)
5. Through the Looking Glass (2001–2015)
6. Post-Genome (2015–)

The visual sequence condenses that arc into the discovery of discrete heredity,
the material gene and genetic code, recombinant DNA, human identity and
heritability, the Human Genome Project, and post-genomic editing ethics.

## Editorial guardrails

- Genetic influence is not presented as genetic determinism.
- Eugenics is identified as coercive misuse, not a scientific conclusion.
- Gene editing is framed through treatment, enhancement, consent, privacy, and
  distribution of benefits.
- Mukherjee's family history is treated as a narrative bridge between genetics
  and lived experience, without diagnosing individuals.
