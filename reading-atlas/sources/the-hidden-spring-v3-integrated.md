# The Hidden Spring — V3 integrated layout source notes

## Edition and structure

- English: *The Hidden Spring: A Journey to the Source of Consciousness* by Mark Solms (2021).
- Korean: 《의식은 어디에서 오는가: 숨겨진 의식의 샘을 찾아서》, 마크 솜즈 지음, 려원기·장현우·이도현 옮김, 하나의학사 (2024).
- The infographic groups the book's 12 chapters into four reading movements: chapters 1–4 (evidence and conceptual turn), 5–6 (feeling and source), 7–9 (free energy and consciousness), and 10–12 (cortex, hard problem, and artificial mind).

## Interpretive model used in the graphic

- The poster presents Solms's proposed brainstem-first account: affective feeling, arousal, and homeostatic value are centered in upper-brainstem systems, while cortex supplies detailed perceptual contents, memory, and predictive models.
- The homeostatic loop is summarized as bodily need → feeling → action → change → prediction-error update.
- The free-energy / active-inference panel distinguishes internal and external states with a Markov-blanket boundary and paired perception/action arrows.
- The graphic explicitly labels the brainstem-first account as a contested hypothesis rather than scientific consensus.
