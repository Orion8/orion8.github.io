## Verified structure and edition history

The official Oxford University Press and author pages list 13 chapters: Why are people?; The replicators; Immortal coils; The gene machine; Aggression; Genesmanship; Family planning; Battle of the generations; Battle of the sexes; You scratch my back, I'll ride on yours; Memes; Nice guys finish first; and The long reach of the gene.

The 1976 first edition contained chapters 1–11. The 1989 second edition added chapters 12 and 13. The 2016 fourth edition marks the 40th anniversary.

## Editorial interpretation

The poster follows the book's gene's-eye arc: replicators use bodies as vehicles; gene-frequency consequences can explain conflict and altruism; kin selection is summarized with Hamilton's rule rB > C; repeated interaction can stabilize conditional cooperation; cultural replicators are introduced as memes; and the extended phenotype carries gene effects beyond the body.

The word “selfish” is explicitly presented as a metaphor about differential replication, not conscious intention, moral character, or inevitable selfishness of organisms. This clarification follows Dawkins's own public explanation.
